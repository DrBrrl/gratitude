Use case: ui-mockup.
Asset type: high-fidelity mobile interface design board for Gratitude, an existing private gratitude journal. This is an onboarding proposal for human review.
Create exactly THREE complete upright mobile app canvases side by side on a landscape board, roughly 390 x 844 CSS-pixel proportions per canvas. Large, legible UI typography, generous but practical spacing. Thin rounded canvas edges; no phone hardware, perspective, hands or browser chrome. Label each canvas with its supplied screen ID and short title OUTSIDE and ABOVE its edge. No watermark or additional screens.
Visual system: dark mode only. Near-black #0c0d10 background with organic charcoal-grey ink suspended in water, soft billows and delicate tendrils; mostly neutral, with only faint warm-yellow illumination in a few existing lit areas. NOT gold smoke, neon, aurora, flames, colourful haze or a flat gradient. Mostly opaque smoked glass text-bearing cards, frosted panels, fine muted yellow borders, subtly rounded corners. Bold white serif page headings like DejaVu Serif, clear off-white/grey sans-serif body and control text like DejaVu Sans. Bright yellow #ffe100 for active marks, primary buttons with near-black labels, and small selected controls. Secondary buttons are translucent light-grey glass with thin subdued yellow borders. Small grey Back arrow text, matching the existing app's settings-back control. Small four-point yellow star AFTER the Gratitude wordmark, to its right and vertically centered, whenever the wordmark is shown; no five-point stars. No extra decorative iconography.
All onboarding controls must fit the viewport; long content scrolls within the page above a visible frosted footer. Use readable body text, not microscopic text. Bottom actions have comfortable mobile touch targets. NO Today/Journal/Friends/Settings tab bar during onboarding. An onboarding top line has a small Back control and the supplied step count. Continue and Skip this question are distinct; steps are optional. Preserve the exact quoted copy, spelling and punctuation. Do not invent explanatory claims or additional fields. Render functional-looking controls and clear selected/unselected states.
